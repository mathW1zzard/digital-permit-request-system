Defect S1 (staging configuration), written before the fix, 2026-11-15 17:40 UTC.

Observation: workers did not crash (no restarts during the run). nginx logged 79 "upstream timed out
while reading response header" (504 after 30 s) followed by 420 "no live upstreams" (502).

Hypothesis: nginx.conf enabled keep-alive connections to the FastCGI workers ("keepalive 8" in the
upstream and "fastcgi_keep_conn on"). php-cgi on Windows handles one connection at a time and keeps
a kept-alive connection open while idle. When nginx opens a second connection to the same worker,
that connection waits in the listen queue until the 30 s read timeout. Each timeout marks the worker
as failed for 10 s (max_fails=1 by default); once all four are marked, nginx answers 502 at once.

Fix: remove "keepalive" from the upstream and "fastcgi_keep_conn on" from the locations, so each
request uses its own connection that php-cgi closes when it is done. Also allow 3 failures in 10 s
before a worker is taken out (max_fails=3), so one slow request does not remove a worker.

Prediction for the rerun (same prefill model, fresh database): 0 timeouts, 0 x 502/504,
1,200 x 201, sign-ins all 200. Submit p95 well under 1 s.
